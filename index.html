Security and control

Your repo. Your merge button.

Prompt Bar is a contributor, not an administrator. The safeguards you already have are the safeguards it works inside.

  • Draft PRs only. Nothing opens ready for review or merges itself.
  • Your repo, your branches. Work happens on a branch, never on your default branch.
  • You merge. CI, approvals and branch protection all still apply.
  • Readable history. Every change is a commit with a real message, co-authored.

FAQ

Questions a reviewer will ask

Can it push straight to production?

No. Changes land as draft pull requests on a branch. Merging is a human action, subject to the same CI and approvals as any other PR.

What if it picks the wrong service?

It reads your project's services, repos and domains before it edits anything, and tells you which one it chose. If the choice is wrong, say so and it moves.

Can I see the change before it becomes a PR?

Yes. Each change runs on its own machine with a preview URL. Ask for another pass while you are looking at it.

Who on the team needs a seat?

Anyone who writes prompts. Reviewing and merging happens in GitHub, so reviewers do not need a Prompt Bar seat.

What happens when we hit the free limit?

Hobby stops at 50 prompts a month and waits for the next month. Nothing is deleted and nothing is charged without you upgrading.

Does it work with our existing repo?

It works with any GitHub repo you can grant access to. There is nothing to migrate and no change to your build.
