Four beats, start to shipped

Click through the flow. The demo above follows along.

Say what you want changed

Type it the way you'd say it to a teammate. Prompt Bar reads your project — services, repos, domains — and works out which one you meant, so "the pricing page" lands in the right codebase.

Watch it build on a live preview

The change runs on its own machine with its own URL. Open it, click around, ask for another pass — nothing touches your main branch until you say so.

Get a pull request, not a surprise

Every change lands as a draft PR on your repo, co-authored and reviewable: real commits, a readable diff, and your own CI running against it.

Merge when you're happy

You merge. Your existing pipeline deploys it, exactly as it would for any other PR. Prompt Bar stays out of the last mile.
